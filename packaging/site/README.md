# Stock Terminal

A free stock research app for your own computer: the financials of every US listed company, a screener,
side-by-side charts, watchlists and alerts, on free public data (SEC EDGAR, Yahoo Finance).

**Download:** https://nielstroep5-creator.github.io/stock-terminal-releases/ or the newest version under
[Releases](https://github.com/nielstroep5-creator/stock-terminal-releases/releases).

This repository only holds the downloads (installers, release notes and the S&P 500 starter pack). Problems:
Settings > About > Report a problem in the app, or open an issue here.

All numbers come from free public sources and are processed automatically, so they can be late or wrong.
Nothing in this app is investment advice. MIT licence.
